Frequently Asked Questions

Is solar still worth it without the 30% tax credit?
Yes. Rising utility rates mean solar still saves many homeowners tens of thousands over 25 years.
What is NEM 3.0 and how does it affect me?
NEM 3.0 reduced payouts for exporting power, making batteries and self-consumption more valuable.
Can I really get solar with $0 down?
Often yes—loans, leases, and PPAs can offer $0 down options depending on qualification.
What credit score do I need?
Many solar loans prefer ~650+. Some lease/PPA options may accept lower.
How long does installation take?
Typically 1–3 months including permits and utility approval.
What happens if I move?
Solar can increase home value; systems can be transferred or paid off at sale.
