Why your electric bill keeps going up

Southern California utility rates keep rising. SCE has increased rates at roughly 6.5% per year.

That means the bill you have today is very likely to be meaningfully higher in the next few years — even if your usage stays the same.

The simple truth:

You can’t negotiate your utility rate. You can only pay it… or reduce dependence on it.

Solar still makes sense (for many homes)

Even if incentives change, many homeowners can still make solar work with the right system design and financing structure.

What solar can do for you

  • Predictability: replace an unpredictable bill with a predictable payment.
  • Lower monthly cost: in many cases $0 down options can be lower than the current bill.
  • Peak protection: batteries can reduce exposure to 4–9pm peak rates.
  • Optionality: once you know your numbers, you can decide when to act.
